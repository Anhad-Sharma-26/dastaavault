DastaaVault — Indian Document Reviewer & Statutory Risk Analyzer
भारतीय दस्तावेज़ समीक्षक

Built for the BharatCode Build League 2026 Hackathon.
Powered by BharatCode Model API (qwen-3.8-27b).

DastaaVault is a zero-cloud-storage, deterministic statutory intelligence
platform designed to protect Indian citizens, MSMEs, borrowers, homebuyers,
and policyholders from hidden contractual traps and regulatory penalties.

Covered Statutory Domains (6 Packs):
1. Income Tax Notices: 1961 Act ↔ 2025 Act concordance, TDS mismatches,
   Section 148 reassessment risks, and unfilled statutory templates.
2. Rent Agreements: Lock-in traps, security deposit forfeiture, and
   Registration Act Section 17 12-month lease thresholds.
3. Banking & Credit (Wave 1): RBI floating loan foreclosure fee bans,
   mandatory Key Fact Statement (KFS) APR disclosures, and title return rules.
4. Business & MSME Defense (Wave 2): CGST Act Section 74 vs 73 fraud SCNs,
   Section 75(4) mandatory hearings, MSMED Section 15 45-day credit ceilings,
   and Section 43B(h) buyer corporate tax disallowance risks.
5. Real Estate & Property (Wave 3): Supreme Court Suraj Lamp (2011) GPA ban,
   RERA Section 18 / Rule 15 penalty parity, and Section 2(k) carpet area rules.
6. Health Insurance (Wave 3): 1% daily room rent proportionate deduction
   haircut math, and Section 45 Insurance Act 3-year moratorium protections.

Privacy Guarantee:
- Client-side PII masking (PAN, Aadhaar, Phone, Email) before transmission.
- Zero server database or document storage. Files exist only in volatile memory.
- Multilingual explanations in English, हिंदी, and Hinglish.
- 18 verified one-click test presets with a 37-test automated verification suite.
