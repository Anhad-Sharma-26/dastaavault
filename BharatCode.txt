DastaaVault — Your Document, Decoded

Built for the BharatCode Build League hackathon.

Uses the BharatCode API (qwen-3.8-27b) to analyze Indian income tax notices
and rent agreements. Extracts structured data, flags risks, computes deadlines,
and explains everything in plain English, Hindi, or Hinglish.

Nothing is stored. PII is masked before any model call.
This is not legal advice.
