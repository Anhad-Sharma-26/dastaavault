Clause 14 — Default, Delay Compensation & Interest Charges
14.1 Buyer Delay Interest: In the event of default or delay by the Allottee in payment of any installment as per the Construction Linked Plan (CLP), the Allottee shall be liable to pay compound interest at the rate of 18.0% per annum compounded monthly for each day of default until complete clearance of arrears.
14.2 Promoter Delay Compensation: In the event the Promoter fails to offer possession of the Unit by the agreed date (31st December 2026 plus grace period), the Promoter shall pay delay compensation calculated strictly at Rs. 5/- (Rupees Five only) per sq.ft. of Super Built-Up Area per month (approx 0.6% per annum). The Allottee shall not be entitled to withhold any installments on grounds of construction delay.
Clause 21 — Measurement & Carpet Area Ambiguity
21.1 Carpet Area Variance: The sale price has been calculated on the Super Built-Up Area of 1,850 sq.ft. The final net usable Carpet Area as defined under Section 2(k) of RERA shall be communicated upon completion of interior plaster work. Any variance of up to 10% in carpet area or common area loading (approx 35% loading factor) shall be accepted by the Allottee without price adjustment.
Clause 23 — Title Conveyance & General Power of Attorney
23.1 Mode of Transfer: The Promoter shall execute final conveyance in favor of the Allottee. In the event full statutory stamp duty or registration clearance is pending from the competent authority, the Promoter may at its sole option execute an irrevocable General Power of Attorney (GPA), Agreement to Sell, and Will transferring possessory rights to the Allottee in lieu of a registered Sale Deed.
Clause 27 — Subvention & Tripartite Financing Indemnity
27.1 Tripartite Indemnity: Under the 10:90 bank subvention scheme with HDFC Housing Finance, the Allottee acknowledges that the loan is sanctioned in the Allottee's personal name. If the Developer ceases or delays payment of pre-EMI subvention interest to the financial institution due to force majeure, the Allottee shall fully indemnify the Developer and remain primarily and solely responsible for all bank dues, interest, and legal costs.
