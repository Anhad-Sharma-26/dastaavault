TENANCY / LEASE AGREEMENT

Bengaluru, Karnataka

This Agreement is entered into on 10 October 2026 between MR. S. RAMANATHAN (Landlord) and MR. KARTIK DESHMUKH (Tenant) for the property situated at Flat 4B, Palm Grove Heights, HSR Layout Sector 2, Bengaluru - 560102.

1. PERIOD OF TENANCY: The tenancy is for a duration of 11 months beginning 15 October 2026.

2. RENT: The agreed monthly rent is ₹25,000/- per month payable on or before the 5th of every month.

3. SECURITY DEPOSIT: The Tenant has paid a sum of ₹75,000/- (Three months rent) as security deposit.

4. LOCK-IN & TERMINATION (CRITICAL CLAUSE):
Both parties agree to a mandatory LOCK-IN PERIOD OF 6 (SIX) MONTHS. Under no circumstances may the Tenant vacate or terminate this agreement during the initial six months. If the Tenant terminates or abandons the premises within the lock-in period, the entire security deposit of ₹75,000/- shall be forfeited unconditionally, and the Tenant shall remain liable to pay the balance rent for the remainder of the lock-in term.
After the expiry of six months, the agreement may be terminated by either party by giving only 1 (ONE) MONTH'S NOTICE in writing.

5. RENT ESCALATION: If renewed, the rent shall increase by 8% for the subsequent term.

6. MAINTENANCE: Common area maintenance of ₹3,500/- per month shall be paid by Tenant.
