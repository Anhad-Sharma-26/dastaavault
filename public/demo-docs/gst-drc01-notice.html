Brief Facts and Grounds of Show Cause Notice

During scrutiny of returns under Section 61 of the CGST Act 2017 for the Financial Year 2022-23, a substantial discrepancy was noticed between the Input Tax Credit (ITC) availed in Form GSTR-3B and the credit reflected in Form GSTR-2B. The taxpayer has availed excess ITC amounting to ₹1,85,000 without corresponding supplier tax invoice remittance. In terms of Section 74(1), it appears that the taxpayer has deliberately claimed excess ITC with willful intent to evade tax.
