This Rent Agreement is made and executed on this 1st day of November, 2026 at Noida, Uttar Pradesh, by and between:

SHRI MOHAN LAL KAPOOR, residing at Flat 301, Green Valley Apartments, Sector 62, Noida (hereinafter called the "LANDLORD" / First Party), which expression shall include his heirs and assigns.

AND

SHRI ANKIT JOSHI, permanent resident of Dehradun, Uttarakhand (hereinafter called the "TENANT" / Second Party), which expression shall include his heirs and assigns.

WHEREAS the Landlord is the absolute owner of Flat No. 302, Green Valley Apartments, Sector 62, Noida - 201309 (hereinafter referred to as the "Premises"), and has agreed to let out the same to the Tenant on the following mutually agreed terms:

1. DURATION: This agreement is made for a term of 11 (eleven) months commencing from 01 November 2026 to 30 September 2027.
2. MONTHLY RENT: The Tenant agrees to pay a monthly rent of ₹18,000/- (Rupees Eighteen Thousand Only) payable in advance on or before the 7th of each English calendar month. Society maintenance charges of ₹2,000/- are separate and payable directly by Tenant.
3. SECURITY DEPOSIT: The Tenant has deposited an interest-free refundable security deposit of ₹36,000/- (Rupees Thirty-Six Thousand Only), equivalent to 2 months' rent, refundable at the time of vacating the premises after adjusting any unpaid utility bills or actual physical damages.
4. LOCK-IN & NOTICE PERIOD: Both parties agree to a lock-in period of 3 (three) months. After expiry of the lock-in period, either party may terminate this agreement by serving 2 (two) months' prior written notice to the other party.
5. ESCALATION: In the event of renewal of this agreement after 11 months with mutual consent, the monthly rent shall be escalated by 5% (five percent).
6. USAGE & MAINTENANCE: The premises shall be used exclusively for residential purposes by the Tenant and his immediate family members.
