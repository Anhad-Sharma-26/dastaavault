This Agreement is made on 01 September 2026 between RAJINDER SINGH HOODA (Owner/Lessor) and NIKHIL CHOPRA (Tenant/Lessee) regarding House No. 142, Sector 45, Gurugram.

Duration: 11 months commencing 01-09-2026 to 31-07-2027.

CLAUSE 7: DEPOSIT FORFEITURE AND NON-REFUND POLICY
The security deposit of ₹45,000/- shall be forfeited in its entirety with zero refund under any of the following circumstances:
(a) If the Tenant vacates before the completion of 11 full months for any reason whatsoever.
(b) If there is any minor delay in monthly rent payment exceeding 5 days.
(c) If any wall marks, appliance wear, or minor damage is noted during joint inspection.
Under no circumstances shall partial refund be permitted or entertained.

Escalation: Annual increment of 5% in monthly rent upon mutual renewal.
