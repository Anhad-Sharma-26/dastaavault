LEASE DEED

Pune, Maharashtra

This Lease Deed is executed on 1st August 2026 between ANAND KULKARNI (Lessor) and ROHIT DESHPANDE (Lessee) for premises situated at Flat 704, Silver Oak Towers, Kothrud, Pune - 411038.

1. TERM OF LEASE: The Lessor hereby demises unto the Lessee the said premises for a fixed tenure of 12 (TWELVE) FULL CALENDAR MONTHS commencing from 01 August 2026 to 31 July 2027.

2. RENT & CHARGES: The agreed monthly lease rental is ₹30,000/- (Thirty Thousand Only) per month plus society maintenance charges.

3. SECURITY DEPOSIT: Lessee has paid an interest-free refundable deposit of ₹90,000/- (equivalent to 3 months rent).

4. NOTICE & TERMINATION: Lock-in period of 6 months. Thereafter, termination with 2 months written notice.

5. RENT ESCALATION: Rent shall be subject to 7% escalation upon each annual rollover.

[Note: This deed is executed on an ordinary ₹100 stamp paper; registration before the Sub-Registrar of Assurances has not been initiated.]

Lessor: Anand Kulkarni
Lessee: Rohit Deshpande
